# Behavior gap analysis for F0–F4, second pass (dirty side, 2026-11-16)

Brief-generator branch only. Compares user-visible behavior and quality practices of the
reference product with Lexon F0–F4 (main f3054b7, plus the F4.1 branch where noted).
Everything is restated in our own words. It contains no code, identifiers, schema, SQL,
prompts, or UI text from the reference. Only plain-language requirements may leave this
branch, inside a brief.

## Coverage of this pass

- Reference: every backend endpoint in the account, user, organization, chat, and audit
  areas; the behavior inventory of the settings, onboarding, security, organization,
  assistant, and history screens (from their tests); end-to-end suite areas; the
  operational documents on organization offboarding, error reporting privacy,
  observability, exports, and test depth; and both recorded incidents.
- Lexon: the corresponding endpoints, screens, and tests on main, checked feature by
  feature. Items marked "verified" were confirmed in Lexon code; "to verify" items are
  for the F4.2 spec or the audit.

## A. Proposed for F4.2 (small, high value, inside F0–F4 scope)

| # | Requirement (behavior) | Lexon today |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | When copying an answer fails (clipboard refused), the user is told it did not copy and can retry. | Verified gap: the copy action ignores the failure silently. |
| G2 | Streamed text is revealed at a steady pace instead of uneven bursts; when the stream ends, or an answer is loaded from history, the full text shows at once; the reveal never shows more than was received and stops work when caught up. | Verified gap: text renders exactly as chunks arrive. |
| G3 | Streaming stays cheap: scroll and layout state are not recomputed on every chunk (batched per frame); when a new answer starts, the user's question sits near the top so the answer reads from its beginning. | To verify. Pair with removing the 1 s full-tree polling (F4 review). |
| G4 | Long-conversation stress requirement: a thread with at least 8 long exchanges, a new answer streamed in 100+ chunks, under 4× CPU slowdown, must produce no render-loop warnings and no stuck scroll button. Recorded as a test, not a manual check. | Not covered. The reference shipped a real defect of exactly this kind (an effect re-running on every chunk). |
| G5 | The composer explains why it cannot send: "the answer is still arriving" differs from "the chat is loading"; Enter does nothing in either state; Stop still works while history is loading; whitespace-only pastes are ignored and pasted spacing is preserved. | To verify per state. |
| G6 | Every list or fetch failure is shown as a failure with a retry, never as an empty state (chats, recent, invitations, members, sessions). | To verify per screen (audit theme). |
| G7 | Settings saves that fail keep the user's draft and offer a retry; a failed save reverts only the setting that failed and says why. | To verify (profile, appearance, organization general). |

## C. Verified parity (no action)

- Remote images in answers are never loaded (Lexon renders no images); links open in a new tab with noopener and noreferrer.
- Email change, two-factor with backup codes, session list and revocation, password reset.
- Pending invitations during onboarding, with retry and expired-invitation handling.
- Role-based organization settings sections; safe error copy for failed runs; stream recovery; permanent chat deletion.

## D. Not adopted (existing owner decisions)

- Model picker and bring-your-own provider keys.
- Two chats side by side; loading another chat in place from a chat header menu.
- Third-party connectors in settings before F16 (MCP), web-search settings before F11.
