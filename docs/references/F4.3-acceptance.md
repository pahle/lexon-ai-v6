## Task 16 assertion log (old → new)

Task 16 part B switched the platform console on: the sidebar Platform group
is Accounts and Organizations, `/platform/provisioning` and
`/platform/provisioning/<id>` redirect into the console, `/platform/accounts`
renders the Accounts console, and the Account status form, provisioning
list, provisioning detail and platform card components are deleted. Every
console route sits behind the `PlatformPage` admin gate (shared no-access
page, no console request for non-admins). Line numbers are those of the
files at `836bc8c` (before the switch-over). API-level assertions on
`/api/platform/provisioning` routes are unchanged.
