## Review Focus

1. Long organization/display names and a delayed paginated switcher response: navigation remains usable without clipping, losing focus, or selecting a different tenant (Task 4).
2. An inactive composer reached through Enter, native submit, drag/drop, clipboard files or keyboard activation: no message, picker, adapter call or request occurs (Task 5).
3. Onboarding and auth at 1023/1024px, sidebar at 767/768px: wrappers do not collapse the form or introduce a second main/sidebar (Tasks 3–4).
4. Expiration/demotion/MFA and stale identity responses while a menu or panel is open: protected content still disappears and existing request-ordering safeguards hold (Tasks 4, 7–8).
5. A screenshot captured before fonts/data settle or in a changed rendering environment: no unstable/empty artifact becomes an approved baseline or weakens comparison (Tasks 9–10).
