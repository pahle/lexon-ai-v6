# F4.3 — Platform admin console

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Depends on** | F4.2 (merged 2026-11-21, PR #46) |
| **Design system** | [LEX-DS-001 v3.2](../specs/LEXON-03-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md) §5.5 Platform pages |
| **Visual target** | `docs/design/f4.1-prototype/` revision 16 (to be drawn from the contracts below and approved before planning) |

## Goal

Give platform administrators a console to find and support the accounts and organizations on Lexon. Two modules, **Accounts** and **Organizations**, replace today's two pages: Account status, which needs a user ID, and Provisioning. Platform administrators can search instead of pasting IDs, see the state of an account or organization at a glance, and act on it with the actions listed here. They never see customer content.

## Users and permissions

- **Platform administrator** (F1, two-step verification required): the only role that can open the console or call its API. Everyone else gets the existing platform no-access page, and the API returns 403 `ORG_FORBIDDEN`, exactly as the current platform routes do.
- A platform administrator never becomes a member of an organization and never sees chats, messages, documents, or any other organization content (LEX-ARC-001 §6.2, F1). Break-glass access is out of scope.
- What a platform administrator may see is listed field by field under "Data and contracts". Nothing else is returned.

## Decisions already made (product owner, 2026-11-21)

1. **Accounts:** a searchable list and an account detail page. Actions:
   - disable and re-enable (today's API, now reached from the list or the detail page);
   - sign the account out everywhere;
   - resend the verification email, for unverified accounts only.
2. **Organizations:** one list of all team organizations, active and provisioning, plus an organization detail page.
   - Actions are today's provisioning actions only: provision, resend the owner invitation, cancel provisioning, and replace the owner email.
   - Suspending an organization and changing the owner of an active organization are not in this slice.
3. **Members are visible:** the detail page of an active organization lists its members (name, email, role, joined date).
   - Every time a platform administrator opens that list, it is recorded as an audit event (decision of this brief; see item 9).
4. **Personal workspaces** are not listed as organizations. An account's detail shows only its team memberships.
5. **No history view in F4.3.** Platform actions are already audited (F1). Viewing the audit log is F17.
6. **Prototype first:** revision 16 of the F4.1 prototype draws every state of this slice from the contracts below, and the owner approves it before planning (LEX-DS-001 §5.6).

## In scope

### Navigation

1. The sidebar Platform group gets two items: **Accounts** and **Organizations**. They replace Account status and Provisioning.
   - The old routes redirect to the new pages.
   - The group remains visible only to platform administrators (LEX-DS-001 §5.5 Shell).

### Accounts

2. **Accounts list** (platform list pattern, §5.5 Platform pages):
   - A search box matches name or email: case-insensitive, contains-match, at least 2 characters, debounced.
   - Columns: name, email, status, two-step verification on or off, team count, last active.
   - **Status** is one of: Active, Unverified (email not verified), or Disabled.
   - Rows are sorted by sign-up date, newest first. Paging uses "Load more" with a cursor.
   - The list has loading, empty, no-results, and failure states. A failure is never shown as empty (LEX-DS-001 §5.1).
3. **Account detail:**
   - Header: name, email, status badge.
   - Facts:
     - signed up;
     - email verified (yes or no);
     - two-step verification (on or off);
     - last active;
     - active sessions count.
   - **Teams:** organization name, slug, and role. Each name links to that organization's detail page.
   - **Actions:**
     - Disable or Enable, with a confirmation dialog naming the person. Disabling also signs the account out everywhere, as today.
     - Sign out everywhere, with a confirmation dialog. It revokes all of the account's sessions and bearer tokens.
     - Resend verification email: shown only while the email is unverified; it uses the existing verification email and its rate limit.
   - Each action reports its result as a toast and refreshes the detail.
4. **Limits on actions:**
   - A platform administrator cannot disable or sign out their own account from the console.
   - Disabling the last enabled platform administrator is refused.
   - These rules are enforced by the API, not only hidden in the UI.

### Organizations

5. **Organizations list:**
   - A search box matches name or slug.
   - A state filter offers All, Active, or Provisioning.
   - Columns: name with slug, state badge, members (count), owner (email of the first owner, or the invited owner email while provisioning), created.
   - **Provision organization** opens today's provisioning form in a dialog. The form, validation, and slug-taken error are unchanged (F4.2 `ORG_SLUG_TAKEN`).
   - The list has loading, empty, no-results, and failure states.
6. **Organization detail, provisioning:** today's provisioning detail, unchanged in function: invitation state, expiry, Resend invitation, Cancel provisioning, and Replace owner email.
7. **Organization detail, active:**
   - Facts: name, slug, created date, member count, whether the organization requires two-step verification.
   - **Members:** name, email, role, joined date. Paged if there are more than 50.
   - Each member's name links to that member's account detail.
   - The detail has no actions in this slice.

### Cross-cutting

8. **Search and paging** run on the server. The client never loads every account.
9. **Audit:** every action writes the existing audit event or a new one, with the platform administrator as actor and no organization content:
   - `platform.user_disabled_changed.v1` exists;
   - new: sessions revoked, verification email resent, organization members viewed.

   Viewing the member list records which organization and when, not who was listed.
10. **Rate limits:** the new read endpoints use the authenticated API rate limit, and the two new account actions have their own per-target limits.
11. **Carry-overs from F4.2:**
    - "Create setup account" must work before hydration, or its tests must wait for hydration.
    - Guard the screenshot form fill at `backend/test/e2e/auth-entry.spec.ts:48-58`.

## User flows

1. **Find an account:**
   1. The administrator types part of an email in Accounts and sees the match.
   2. They open the account: status Active, two-step on, two teams.
   3. They follow a team to its organization detail.
2. **Support an unverified user:** search the account, see status Unverified, and click Resend verification email. A toast confirms; a second click within the rate limit is refused with a clear message.
3. **Compromised account:** open the account, click Sign out everywhere, and confirm. The session count drops to 0, and the user's other devices are signed out at their next request.
4. **Disable and re-enable:** the confirmation names the person. After disabling, the status reads Disabled and sessions read 0. Enabling restores sign-in.
5. **Self-protection:** on the administrator's own account, Disable and Sign out everywhere are not offered, and the API refuses them if called.
6. **Provisioning:**
   1. Organizations → Provision organization: the dialog appears, then the new row shows Provisioning.
   2. Open the row and use Resend, Replace owner email, or Cancel, as today.
   3. When the owner accepts, the row shows Active with 1 member.
7. **Active organization:** open it and see facts and members. Opening the member list writes an audit event.
8. **Failure states:** a failed list or detail fetch shows the error row with Try again, never an empty state.
9. **Non-admin:** a member who opens a platform URL sees the no-access page, and the API returns 403.

## Data and contracts

All new routes are under `/api/platform`, need a platform administrator with two-step verification, and validate input with Zod schemas in `shared/` (LEX-TS-001: one Zod). Paging uses the existing `PaginationSchema` and `pageSchema` (`shared/src/pagination.ts`).

| Route | Returns or does |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/platform/accounts?query=&limit=&cursor=` | Page of `PlatformAccountSummary`: `id`, `name`, `email`, `status` (`active` \| `unverified` \| `disabled`), `twoFactorEnabled`, `teamCount`, `createdAt`, `lastActiveAt` (nullable) |
| `GET /api/platform/accounts/:userId` | `PlatformAccountDetail`: the summary plus `emailVerified`, `activeSessionCount`, `teams` [`organizationId`, `name`, `slug`, `role`] |
| `PATCH /api/platform/users/:userId/disabled` | Existing; it gains the self and last-administrator refusals (item 4) |
| `POST /api/platform/accounts/:userId/sessions/revoke` | 204. It revokes all sessions and bearer tokens and refuses self. Audited |
| `POST /api/platform/accounts/:userId/verification-email` | 204. 409 when already verified; 429 inside the rate limit. Audited |
| `GET /api/platform/organizations?query=&state=&limit=&cursor=` | Page of `PlatformOrganizationSummary`: `id`, `name`, `slug`, `state` (`active` \| `provisioning`), `memberCount`, `ownerEmail`, `createdAt` |
| `GET /api/platform/organizations/:organizationId` | `PlatformOrganizationDetail`, a union on `state`. **Active:** summary plus `requireTwoFactor`. **Provisioning:** today's `ProvisioningSummary` fields |
| `GET /api/platform/organizations/:organizationId/members?limit=&cursor=` | Page of `name`, `email`, `role`, `joinedAt`, `userId`. Audited as members viewed |
| Existing `/api/platform/provisioning` routes | Unchanged; the Organizations module calls them |

- **No field beyond these tables.** In particular, no chat titles, message counts, document names, IP addresses, user agents, or session device names.
- **Errors use the existing safe codes.** An unknown account or organization returns 404. Status codes follow F4.2: 409 for conflicts, 503 for dependency outages, never 500.
- **Reading across organizations needs a narrow database path.** It must not widen the tenant role. The spec designs it, for example a platform read role or `SECURITY DEFINER` functions granted only to the platform path, in the style of the F1 `lexon_auth` functions. It is covered by tests proving that `lexon_app` cannot use it.
- **Migrations:** only those this design needs, such as an index for case-insensitive email and name search and the audit event types.

## Non-functional requirements

- **Security:**
  - Every route is negative-tested: a non-admin member gets 403, an admin without two-step verification gets 403, and an anonymous caller gets 401.
  - Self and last-administrator refusals are tested.
  - Responses are tested to contain only the listed fields, with a strict schema.
  - Searches cannot be abused for enumeration by non-admins, because they are behind the admin gate, and they are rate-limited.
- **Privacy:** no K1 content in responses, logs, or traces (LEX-ARC-001 §6). Search terms are not logged.
- **Performance:**
  - List queries stay indexed with at least 10,000 synthetic accounts and 2,000 organizations.
  - The p95 of a search request is recorded on Linux CI; this is not a blocking latency gate on shared runners.
- **Accessibility:** axe passes on every new state in both themes. Tables keep semantic headers, and dialogs keep focus rules.
- **Visual:**
  - Every new state has a revision 16 prototype state and a capture id.
  - Comparison sheets come from CI artifacts only and are reviewed by the owner.
  - The gate is 1.5% (LEX-DS-001 §5.6).
  - The current platform goldens (`platform-accounts`, `platform-provisioning-*`) are retired and replaced; every other golden stays unchanged.
- **Process (standing rules, unchanged from F4.2):**
  - TDD with RED evidence.
  - A draft PR from the first task. Every report states the CI result of its head.
  - Never weaken functional, accessibility, or security assertions; log changed assertions old → new.
  - Docker: only `lexon-e2e-*` by explicit name, never prune.
  - Budget mode: a low-effort manager, at most 2 low-effort workers, at most 2 Linux rounds per area.
  - Any visual re-approval, extra round, or extra smoke run stops for the owner first.
  - Subagents are stopped when their work is done.
